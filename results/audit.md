## Notes on interpretation

- The SMILES column is `IsomericSMILES`, which keeps stereochemistry where the source specifies it. With stereo kept there are 0 duplicate canonical SMILES; with stereo removed, 3522 rows collapse to 3400 structures (110 groups of stereoisomers, 232 rows). The non-chiral Morgan fingerprint used by the baseline cannot tell these rows apart, so random CV keeps each group in one fold (see `results/baseline.md`). In 88 of the 110 stereoisomer groups the rows' label sets differ; labels are used as given.
- The two largest scaffold clusters (acyclic: 1821, `c1ccccc1`: 574) together hold 68.0% of molecules. A Bemis-Murcko split treats all acyclic molecules as one group; see `results/baseline.md` for how the split handles it.
