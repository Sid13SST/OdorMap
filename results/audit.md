## Notes on interpretation

- The SMILES column is `nonStereoSMILES`: stereochemistry was removed upstream, so
  stereoisomers are not distinguishable in this file. "0 duplicate canonical SMILES"
  is measured on these stereo-free strings.
- The empty (acyclic) scaffold and `c1ccccc1` together cover
  the two largest scaffold clusters listed above. A Bemis-Murcko split treats all
  acyclic molecules as one group; see `results/baseline.md` for how the split handles it.
